# zigcho!lazer 1.0.0 and score multipliers

the lazer client dropped the alpha tag and moves to 1.0.0.

the main menu finally drops the ugly yellow hazard dev banner from upstream builds, replaced with a subtle glass brand pill.

score multiplier evaluation is fixed across online leaderboards and tooltips. online scores with custom Difficulty Adjust settings or non-default DT/NC rates now evaluate with zigcho's continuous DA curves and rate scaling instead of getting hit with upstream penalties.

standalone portable packages are up for windows x64, macos arm64, linux x64, and the signed android arm64 apk.
