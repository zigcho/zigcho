# the pp boost is for relax

the balance change was applied too broadly. the 25% baseline and extra mod adjustments now apply to relax only, on stable and lazer.

vanilla, autopilot and ScoreV2 are back on their previous calculations. existing scores and profile stats get rebuilt to remove the extra pp from those modes. relax keeps its boost and the custom DT/NC/HT rate fix. raw scores and hit results are unchanged.
