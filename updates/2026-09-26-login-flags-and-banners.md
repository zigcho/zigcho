# flags and banners should just work

lazer now saves your country on your first login, using the trusted country from the connection. stable and web logins do the same, and the last-login time actually moves when you sign in. once your flag is set, another login will not quietly change it.

admins can change a player's flag from the staff page, with the reason kept in the audit trail. if you have premium, you can change your own flag in settings. the change is shared across the website, stable and lazer.

you can also upload a normal banner image now. the website crops and resizes it to fit instead of making you prepare a 2000 × 500 file first. an animated gif that needs cropping becomes a still image; one that already fits keeps its animation.
