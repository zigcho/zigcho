# less scrolling before you even get anywhere

top plays, recent plays and first places now start with 25 plays each. hit show more to load another 25, then another 25 after that. each list does its own thing, and pinned plays stay as they were.

top-play positions and weighted pp carry on from the previous page. first places still show the full total, even when you have only opened the first 25. your source, mode and privacy settings apply to every page.
